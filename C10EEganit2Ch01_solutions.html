Chapter 1: Similarity

1. Chapter at a Glance

This chapter introduces the concept of similarity in triangles. Students will learn about the ratio of areas of triangles, the Basic Proportionality Theorem, tests for similarity, properties of angle bisectors, and the property of areas of similar triangles. These concepts are fundamental for understanding geometric relationships and solving problems involving proportional lengths and areas.

2. Important Concepts Explained

Ratio of Areas of Two Triangles

The ratio of the areas of two triangles is equal to the ratio of the products of their bases and corresponding heights.

If a triangle has base $b_1$ and height $h_1$, and another has base $b_2$ and height $h_2$, the ratio of their areas is:

$$\frac{A(\triangle_1)}{A(\triangle_2)} = \frac{b_1 \times h_1}{b_2 \times h_2}$$

  • Condition 1 (Equal Heights): If the heights of both triangles are equal ($h_1 = h_2 = h$), then:

$$\frac{A(\triangle_1)}{A(\triangle_2)} = \frac{b_1}{b_2}$$

Property: The ratio of the areas of two triangles with equal heights is equal to the ratio of their corresponding bases.

  • Condition 2 (Equal Bases): If the bases of both triangles are equal ($b_1 = b_2 = b$), then:

$$\frac{A(\triangle_1)}{A(\triangle_2)} = \frac{h_1}{h_2}$$

Property: The ratio of the areas of two triangles with equal bases is equal to the ratio of their corresponding heights.

Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem)

Theorem: If a line parallel to a side of a triangle intersects the remaining sides in two distinct points, then the line divides the sides in the same proportion.

Given $\triangle ABC$ with line $l \parallel$ side $BC$, intersecting $AB$ at $P$ and $AC$ at $Q$:

$$\frac{AP}{PB} = \frac{AQ}{QC}$$

Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem

Theorem: If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

Property of an Angle Bisector of a Triangle

Theorem: The bisector of an angle of a triangle divides the side opposite to the angle in the ratio of the remaining sides.

In $\triangle ABC$, if ray $CE$ bisects $\angle C$ and intersects $AB$ at $E$:

$$\frac{AE}{EB} = \frac{CA}{CB}$$

Property of Three Parallel Lines and Their Transversals

Theorem: The ratio of the intercepts made on a transversal by three parallel lines is equal to the ratio of the corresponding intercepts made on any other transversal by the same parallel lines.

If line $l \parallel$ line $m \parallel$ line $n$ and transversals $t_1$ and $t_2$ intersect them at points $A, B, C$ and $P, Q, R$ respectively:

$$\frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{PQ}{QR}$$

Tests of Similarity of Triangles

  1. AAA Test: If corresponding angles of two triangles are congruent, the triangles are similar.
  2. AA Test: If two angles of one triangle are congruent to two corresponding angles of another triangle, the triangles are similar.
  3. SAS Test: If two pairs of corresponding sides are in the same proportion and the angles between them are congruent, the triangles are similar.
  4. SSS Test: If three sides of a triangle are in proportion to the corresponding three sides of another triangle, the triangles are similar.

Theorem of Areas of Similar Triangles

Theorem: When two triangles are similar, the ratio of areas of those triangles is equal to the ratio of the squares of their corresponding sides.

If $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$:

$$\frac{A(\triangle ABC)}{A(\triangle PQR)} = \frac{AB^2}{PQ^2} = \frac{BC^2}{QR^2} = \frac{AC^2}{PR^2}$$

3. Definitions and Key Terms

  • Similar Triangles: Triangles where corresponding angles are congruent and corresponding sides are in the same proportion.
  • Median: A segment joining a vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side.
  • Intercept: The segment of a transversal cut off by parallel lines.
  • Angle Bisector: A ray that divides an angle into two congruent angles.
  • Concyclic Points: Points that lie on the same circle.

4. Formulas, Rules, and Properties

  • Area of a Triangle: $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base} \times \text{Height}$
  • Reflexivity: $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle ABC$
  • Symmetry: If $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$, then $\triangle DEF \sim \triangle ABC$
  • Transitivity: If $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$ and $\triangle DEF \sim \triangle GHI$, then $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle GHI$

5. Worked Examples — Step by Step

Ex. (1) (Page 12)

Given: In the figure, $AE \perp$ seg $BC$, $DF \perp$ line $BC$, $AE = 4$, $DF = 6$.

Find: $\frac{A(\triangle ABC)}{A(\triangle DBC)}$

Solution:

  1. Both triangles share the same base $BC$.
  2. The ratio of areas of triangles with equal bases is equal to the ratio of their heights.
  3. $\frac{A(\triangle ABC)}{A(\triangle DBC)} = \frac{AE}{DF} = \frac{4}{6}$
  4. Simplifying the fraction: $\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}$

Final Answer: $\frac{2}{3}$

Ex. (2) (Page 13)

Given: In $\triangle ABC$, point $D$ on side $BC$ such that $DC = 6, BC = 15$.

Find: $\frac{A(\triangle ABD)}{A(\triangle ABC)}$ and $\frac{A(\triangle ABD)}{A(\triangle ADC)}$

Solution:

  1. $BC = 15, DC = 6 \implies BD = BC - DC = 15 - 6 = 9$.
  2. Triangles $ABD, ADC, ABC$ have the same height (from vertex $A$ to line $BC$).
  3. $\frac{A(\triangle ABD)}{A(\triangle ABC)} = \frac{BD}{BC} = \frac{9}{15} = \frac{3}{5}$
  4. $\frac{A(\triangle ABD)}{A(\triangle ADC)} = \frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{3}{2}$

Final Answer: $\frac{3}{5}$ and $\frac{3}{2}$

6. Exercise / Practice Set Solutions

Practice Set 1.1

1. Base of a triangle is 9 and height is 5. Base of another triangle is 10 and height is 6. Find the ratio of areas.

  • $A_1 = \frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 5 = 22.5$
  • $A_2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 6 = 30$
  • Ratio = $\frac{22.5}{30} = \frac{225}{300} = \frac{3}{4}$

Final Answer: $3:4$

2. In figure 1.13, $BC \perp AB, AD \perp AB, BC = 4, AD = 8$. Find $\frac{A(\triangle ABC)}{A(\triangle ADB)}$.

  • Both triangles have the same base $AB$.
  • Ratio of areas = Ratio of heights = $\frac{BC}{AD} = \frac{4}{8} = \frac{1}{2}$

Final Answer: $1:2$

3. In figure 1.14, $PS \perp RQ, QT \perp PR$. If $RQ = 6, PS = 6, PR = 12$. Find $QT$.

  • $A(\triangle PQR) = \frac{1}{2} \times RQ \times PS = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 6 = 18$
  • Also, $A(\triangle PQR) = \frac{1}{2} \times PR \times QT = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times QT = 6 \times QT$
  • $6 \times QT = 18 \implies QT = 3$

Final Answer: $3$

4. In figure 1.15, $AP \perp BC, AD \parallel BC$. Find $A(\triangle ABC) : A(\triangle BCD)$.

  • Both triangles have base $BC$.
  • Since $AD \parallel BC$, the height of both triangles is the same (distance between parallel lines).
  • Ratio = $1:1$

Final Answer: $1:1$

9. Quick Revision Sheet

  • Ratio of Areas: $\frac{A_1}{A_2} = \frac{b_1 h_1}{b_2 h_2}$
  • Basic Proportionality Theorem: Parallel line divides sides proportionally.
  • Angle Bisector Theorem: Bisector divides opposite side in ratio of remaining sides.
  • Similarity Tests: AAA, AA, SAS, SSS.
  • Area of Similar Triangles: $\frac{A_1}{A_2} = (\frac{s_1}{s_2})^2$
