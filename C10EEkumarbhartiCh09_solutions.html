1. Chapter at a Glance

“Connecting the Dots” is an extract from Steve Jobs’ address to graduating students. Jobs shares three stories from his life: connecting the dots, love and loss, and death. The stories show how curiosity, setbacks and awareness of limited time shaped his outlook.

  • Connecting the dots: Calligraphy classes he attended after leaving Reed College later influenced the typography of the Macintosh.
  • Love and loss: Being fired from Apple was devastating, but he began again and started NeXT and Pixar. He later returned to Apple.
  • Death: A cancer diagnosis and surgery reminded him that time is limited and that people should not waste it living someone else’s life.

2. Important Concepts Explained

  • Connecting the dots: Experiences may not make sense while they are happening. Their significance can become clear when we look back.
  • Curiosity: Jobs attended a calligraphy class because it interested him. The knowledge later helped him appreciate typefaces and proportional spacing in computer fonts.
  • Resilience: After losing his position at Apple, Jobs started over rather than abandoning work he loved.
  • Follow your heart and intuition: Jobs advises listeners not to let other people’s opinions drown out their own inner voice.
  • Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish: In the speech, this means continuing to seek new things and keeping an open mind rather than assuming you know everything.

5. Say HOW?

(a) How did the calligraphy classes help Steve Jobs after ten years?
He used what he had learned about serif and sans-serif typefaces when designing the first Macintosh. This helped the Mac have multiple typefaces and proportionally spaced fonts.

(b) How can you connect the dots?
You cannot always see how present experiences will connect in the future. Jobs says to trust that they may make sense when you look back, and to trust your gut, destiny, life or karma.

(c) How did Jobs react after the shock of being fired from Apple?
He gradually realised that he still loved what he did. Starting over freed him to enter one of the most creative periods of his life.

(d) How was Jobs cured of a rare cancer?
After a biopsy, doctors found a rare form of pancreatic cancer that was treatable with surgery. He underwent the surgery and said he was fine.

(e) How did Jobs acquire the words “Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish”?
He saw the words on the back cover of the final issue of The Whole Earth Catalogue. He said he had always wished the same for himself and then wished it for the graduates.

10. Speech: “The Will to Win”

The Will to Win

Respected Principal, teachers and my dear friends, good morning.

Today I am going to speak about “The Will to Win.” Winning is not only about receiving a prize; it is about giving our best, learning from failure and refusing to give up.

Every successful person faces difficulties. A student may fail a test, a sportsperson may lose a match, and an inventor may face repeated rejection. What matters is the decision to learn, practise and try again. Steve Jobs’ story teaches us that a setback can sometimes become the beginning of a new opportunity.

To develop the will to win, we should set clear goals, make a plan, practise regularly, manage our time and learn from people who guide us. We should also accept our mistakes honestly and treat others with respect.

This attitude improves our confidence, discipline and ability to solve problems. Success may not come immediately, but steady effort helps us grow.

Let us remember: real victory begins when we decide not to give up. Work hard, stay curious and keep moving towards your goal.

Thank you.

5. Important Questions and Answers

Why did Jobs say that we can connect the dots only by looking backwards?

At the time an experience occurs, we may not understand its importance. Later, when we look back, we may see how it contributed to a future opportunity. Jobs used his calligraphy classes as an example.

What did Jobs learn from being fired from Apple?

He realised that he still loved his work. Beginning again gave him freedom to be a beginner and led to a creative period in which he started NeXT and Pixar.

What warning did Jobs give about time and dogma?

He said that time is limited and should not be wasted living someone else’s life. He warned against being trapped by dogma or letting other people’s opinions drown out one’s own inner voice.

What is the message of “Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish”?

Keep learning, stay curious and remain open to new ideas. Do not become so confident in what you know that you stop exploring.
