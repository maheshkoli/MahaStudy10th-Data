1. Chapter Overview

“Be SMART...!” is a prose lesson about setting goals and working systematically towards success. It explains that desire, dreams, vision, skill and will need to be supported by action. The chapter introduces SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic and Time-bound. It also develops practical skills such as speech writing, planning and English grammar.

Central idea: A dream becomes a practical goal when it is clearly defined, measurable, achievable, realistic and connected with a time limit. Consistent action, planning and perseverance are necessary to turn intentions into results.

3. Complete Explanation of the Prose

Champions, Desire and Will

The lesson begins with the idea that champions are not created merely by physical training. They are made from something deep inside them: a desire, a dream and a vision. They need skill and will, and the text stresses that the will must be stronger than the skill. Successful professionals similarly use their natural talents and take action to achieve their goals.

Importance of Goal Setting

Goals give direction to life. They act as a stepping stone that helps illuminate the path and put a person “at the helm” of life. Instead of simply drifting and letting things happen, a person who sets goals can steer a course towards a desired future.

Why Goal Setting Can Be Difficult

Setting goals may look easy, but it can be challenging because a person has to decide what they finally want to achieve. The fear of failure can also make people avoid setting goals. The lesson therefore introduces a five-step SMART approach.

What SMART Means

LetterWordMeaning in the lesson
SSpecificIdentify a clearly defined area or target.
MMeasurableQuantify the goal so progress can be tracked and benchmarked.
AAchievableSet a goal that can be completed within the designated period and can serve as a stepping stone.
RRealisticKeep the goal within the current skill set, expertise and available reality.
TTime-boundSet time parameters or a deadline for the goal.

Specific

A specific goal identifies exactly what needs to be improved or achieved. The more specific the area, the easier it is to define parameters and work towards the result.

Measurable

Quantifying a goal provides a way to track progress. It makes it possible to benchmark performance throughout the goal period and identify areas where improvement is needed.

Achievable

An achievable goal can be completed in the designated period. Smaller achievable goals can act as stepping stones towards broader career goals. The lesson uses the example that students may aspire to high positions in the future but need to work towards them step by step.

Realistic

A realistic goal should fit a person's current skill set or area of expertise. Building expertise takes time, so expecting to become an expert in a very short time is unrealistic. A clear picture of the desired goal and the ability to adhere to it are important.

Time-bound

Every goal needs time parameters. A deadline increases focus and accountability. The lesson explains that exercise, diet and other actions become effective only when they are practised consistently within a defined period.

From Dreams to Action

Visions, wishes, intentions and dreams stimulate imagination and help people decide where they want to reach. But they must be brought down to earth and converted into strategies that can be executed. The quality and quantity of energy put into the work directly affect the results.

“If we don’t put anything in, we can’t get anything out.”

Success as a Walk in the Dark

The lesson compares success to a walk in the dark. A person must find the right footing, master skills and take the next step carefully. The next step should support the person without a crack. This emphasizes careful planning, action and steady progress.

5. Textbook Questions and Answers

Q1. How does quantifying goals help us?

Quantifying goals gives specific ways to track progress. It makes it easier to benchmark performance during the goal period and identify areas that need improvement.

Q2. Why do some people find setting goals a challenge?

Setting goals can be challenging because a person must decide what they want to achieve in the end. The fear of failure may also cause some people to avoid setting goals.

Q3. What factor is unreal while setting goals?

Expecting to become an expert in a short period of time is unrealistic. A goal that is far beyond one's current skills or area of expertise without a practical path is also unrealistic.

Q4. To what are efforts towards success compared? Why?

Efforts towards success are compared to a walk in the dark. A person has to find the right footing, master the required skills and take the next step carefully while approaching and tackling problems.

8. Speech — “How to Achieve Success in Life”

Respected judges, teachers and my dear friends,

Good morning to everyone. Today I am going to speak on the topic “How to Achieve Success in Life.”

Success does not happen by accident. We need a clear goal, proper planning, knowledge, confidence, hard work and perseverance. First, we should decide what we want to achieve. Then we should divide the goal into smaller, achievable steps.

We should also use the SMART method. Our goals should be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic and Time-bound. Proper guidance, dedication and learning from successful examples can help us improve. Failures should not make us give up; instead, they should teach us what to change.

We must remember that dreams become meaningful only when we act on them. As the lesson says, success is like a walk in the dark: we need to find the right footing and take the next step carefully.

So, set a goal, make a plan, work hard, learn from mistakes and never give up.

Thank you.

10. Model Long Answers

Q. Explain the SMART methodology in detail.

SMART is a goal-setting methodology consisting of five qualities: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic and Time-bound. A specific goal clearly identifies what is to be achieved. A measurable goal can be quantified so that progress can be tracked. An achievable goal can be completed within the available period and can serve as a stepping stone. A realistic goal matches the person's present skills, resources and expertise. A time-bound goal has a definite time parameter or deadline. Together these qualities turn a general wish into a practical plan of action.

Q. Explain the importance of goal setting.

Goal setting gives direction to life. It helps a person decide what needs to be achieved instead of simply letting things happen. Goals can provide a sense of originality and act as stepping stones towards a better future. They help a person organise effort, track progress and remain accountable. However, goals should be practical and supported by consistent action.

Q. Explain the statement “Success is a walk in the dark.”

The statement compares success with walking through darkness because the path is not always obvious. A person has to find the right footing, master the required skills, tackle problems and carefully take the next step. Success therefore requires planning, judgement, learning and steady progress rather than simply wishing for a result.
