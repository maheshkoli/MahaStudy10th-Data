1. Lesson at a Glance

Subject: Stephen Hawking (8 January 1942–14 March 2018), British theoretical physicist, cosmologist and author.

The lesson describes Hawking’s work in physics and cosmology, his books for general readers, and the effects of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) on his life. Although his physical abilities declined, he continued to work and communicate using assistive technology. The text presents his persistence and scientific curiosity as central features of his life.

6. “Every cloud has a silver lining.” Explain with reference to Hawking.

The proverb means that even in a difficult situation, something hopeful or positive may emerge. Stephen Hawking was diagnosed with ALS at the age of 21, and doctors initially gave him a very short life expectancy. Although his physical condition gradually worsened, he devoted himself to research and writing. With the help of assistants and communication technology, he continued to share scientific ideas with the world. His life shows that physical difficulties did not prevent him from making important contributions to science.

10. Nouns and determiners

Key: Determiners are in yellow; nouns are bold.

  1. He lost his voice for good.
  2. Over the years, Hawking has written 15 books.
  3. These three books articulate his search for science’s Holy Grail.
  4. A few events prevented him from despondency.
  5. That dream made him realize it.

Some school grammar classifications treat numerals such as “15” and “three” as determiners; “science’s” is a possessive noun modifying “Holy Grail.”

12. Film review: The Theory of Everything

Title: The Theory of Everything

Characters: Stephen Hawking, Jane Hawking and members of their family and academic community.

Story: The film portrays Stephen Hawking’s student years, his relationship with Jane, his diagnosis with motor neurone disease and his continuing scientific career.

Dialogues and acting: The performances communicate the characters’ emotions and the challenges of living with a progressive illness.

Picturisation and music: The film uses university settings, family scenes and a reflective musical score to support its emotional tone.

Special features: It presents aspects of Hawking’s life and scientific work for a broad audience.

Opinion: It is an inspiring biographical drama that highlights determination, relationships and intellectual curiosity.

Message: Difficult circumstances may change a person’s life, but they do not necessarily end the possibility of learning, achievement and contribution.
